About Me

Spatial Data Science Meets Real-World Impact

I'm Hasibul Islam — a GIS & Geospatial Analytics Specialist with 14 years of experience applying spatial data science to conservation, public health, disaster management, and international development. Currently serving as a Consultant at The World Bank in Bangladesh, I lead geospatial monitoring, data pipeline development, and digital transformation initiatives across South Asia.

My career spans six organisations — from mapping elephant corridors for IUCN to monitoring 45 health facilities for WHO in Cox's Bazar, from leading flood risk assessments for German Red Cross to building portfolio-wide analytics platforms for the World Bank covering Bangladesh, Bhutan, and Pakistan.

I combine deep GIS expertise with modern tools — Power BI dashboards, KoboToolbox data systems, Python automation, and Generative AI — to turn complex data into decisions that matter on the ground.
